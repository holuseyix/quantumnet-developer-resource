03 · WHAT TO KNOW
!

Experimental means experimental.

Quantumnet is an engineering playground, not a production network. The official announcement says its UX is intentionally limited and that the current focus is integrating and evaluating core components.

Future iterations may change security analysis, performance, functionality and tooling.

03

WHAT TO KNOW

Experimental means experimental.

Quantumnet is currently an engineering playground, not a production network. The official announcement says its UX is intentionally limited and that the current focus is integrating and evaluating the core components.

Future iterations may add security analysis, performance improvements, functionality and better tooling.

Contribution principle

Separate current facts from open questions. Link to primary sources. Don't turn an experimental implementation into a claim about production readiness.
